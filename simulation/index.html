WAVE DESCRIPTION

An ECG signal represents the electrical activity of the heart, showing the depolarization and repolarization of the atria and ventricles.

Instructions

1. Signal Acquisition: Select a dataset (ECG 1, 2, or 3) from the Input Panel and click on Load Raw Signal button. The raw (noisy) signal will appear in red.
2. Pre-processing: Click on Filter Signal button. This applies a Butterworth filter to remove baseline wander and high-frequency noise. The clean signal (blue) will appear below.
3. Signal Analysis: Choose one of the two methods below to identify wave components.
A. Auto Analysis (Algorithmic)

Click on . The system will automatically:

  • Detect R-peaks (High precision).
  • Highlight P-wave, QRS, and T-wave boundaries.
  • Calculate Heart Rate and Intervals in the Results Panel.
B. Manual Labelling (Interactive)

Sequence: P-Start → P-End → QRS-Start → QRS-End → T-Start → T-End

Click on . You must click on the graph 6 times in this exact order:

  1. P-Wave Onset (Start)
  2. P-Wave Offset (End)
  3. QRS Onset (Start)
  4. QRS Offset (End)
  5. T-Wave Onset (Start)
  6. T-Wave Offset (End)

Once finished, a "Cross-Check" button will appear to compare your marks against the algorithm.

4. Navigation & Details:
  • Snap-to-Beat: Use the scrollbar to jump between individual heartbeats.
  • Hover: Move your mouse over any colored wave component to see its medical description and duration.
5. Reset: Click on Reset button to clear all data and start a new experiment.
